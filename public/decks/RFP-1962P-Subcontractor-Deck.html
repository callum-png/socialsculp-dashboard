RFP #1962P

Subcontractor Responsibilities
Waste Disposal Company
City of Alexandria, Louisiana

Overview

The Waste Disposal Subcontractor is engaged by the Prime Contractor to handle the actual transportation, treatment, storage, disposal, and reclamation of collected household hazardous waste materials.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • The Subcontractor does NOT directly contract with the City
  • All work is through the Prime Contractor
  • Payment comes from Prime Contractor, not directly from City
  • All documentation must be provided to Prime Contractor

Facility Qualifications

Required Permits & Qualifications

  • EPA permitted TSDF (Treatment, Storage, Disposal Facility)
  • Current RCRA Part B Permit
  • Ability to accept materials shipped on hazardous waste manifest

Pre-Event Responsibilities

  • Provide facility information to Prime Contractor for inclusion in proposal
  • Ensure all permits are current and valid
  • Provide pricing to Prime Contractor for:
    • Transportation charges
    • Disposal/recycling charges per pound
    • Fuel surcharges (if applicable)
    • Container charges
    • Any minimum charges
  • Prepare hazardous waste manifests for all shipments
  • Ensure manifests comply with EPA and state regulations

Post-Event Responsibilities

1. Material Transportation

  • Pick up materials from collection site
  • Transport materials using proper hazardous waste vehicles
  • Ensure all DOT regulations are followed
  • Use appropriate containers and packaging
  • Maintain chain of custody documentation

2. Material Processing

Recycling: Process recyclable materials
Treatment: Treat corrosive and oxidizer materials
Incineration: Incinerate appropriate materials
Fuel Blend: Process flammable materials for fuel blending
Landfill: Dispose of non-recyclable, non-hazardous materials (last resort)

Documentation & Reporting

⚠️ Critical Deadline: 30 Days

All documentation must be provided to Prime Contractor within 30 days of collection

Certificates of Recycling and/or Disposal

Certificates must detail:

  • Material types processed
  • Quantities processed
  • Disposal/recycling methods used
  • Facility information
  • Date of processing

Hazardous Waste Manifest

  • Must be complete and accurate
  • Must show proper chain of custody
  • Must comply with EPA and state regulations

⚠️ Important: City will not pay Prime Contractor until certificates and manifests are received. This directly impacts your payment from Prime Contractor.

Regulatory Compliance

Compliance Requirements

EPA regulations - Full compliance required
RCRA requirements - Current Part B Permit
State regulations - Louisiana DEQ compliance
DOT regulations - Transportation compliance

Quality Assurance

Processing Standards

  • Process materials according to best available methods
  • Prioritize recycling over disposal
  • Ensure maximum recovery of recyclable materials
  • Minimize landfill disposal

Environmental Compliance

  • Operate in full compliance with all environmental regulations
  • Prevent releases to environment
  • Properly manage any waste generated from processing
  • Maintain facility in good standing with regulators

⚠️ Critical: Any regulatory violations could affect Prime Contractor's contract and your relationship with them.
