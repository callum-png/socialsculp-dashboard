1. Purpose of This Addendum

This document is submitted as an addendum to the formal bid and price quotation for RFP #1962P (Household Hazardous Waste Transporting, Disposal and Reclamation Service). Its purpose is to provide the City of Alexandria with full transparency regarding the structure of the proposing team, the role of SocialSculp LLC in the procurement process, and the commitment of Clean Management Environmental Group, Inc. (“CMEG”) as the prime contractor that will perform all work under any resulting contract.

The City is invited to review this addendum alongside the bid and quote for a complete picture of our team and our approach to this opportunity.

2. Team Structure — Prime Contractor & Procurement Agent

2.1 Prime Contractor — Performance of Work

Clean Management Environmental Group, Inc. (CMEG) is the prime contractor on this proposal. CMEG will:

  • Perform all household hazardous waste collection, transportation, disposal, and reclamation services required by the RFP.
  • Fulfill every technical, safety, regulatory, and operational requirement set forth in the specifications (staffing, OSHA Hazwoper-trained personnel, health and safety plans, PPE, equipment, manifests, recycling/disposal at permitted facilities, etc.).
  • Execute any contract awarded by the City in CMEG’s name and bear full responsibility for performance, quality, safety, and compliance.
  • Hold and maintain all required licenses, permits, and certifications under Louisiana and federal law (including RCRA and LDEQ requirements).

All pricing, qualifications, references, insurance, and technical content in the bid and quote are those of CMEG. The City’s contractual relationship for performance will be with CMEG as the awarded contractor.

2.2 Procurement Agent — Bid Preparation & Submission

SocialSculp LLC is a procurement and bid management company that has partnered with CMEG for this opportunity. SocialSculp’s role is limited to:

  • Identifying and monitoring government and municipal contracting opportunities that match CMEG’s capabilities.
  • Preparing the bid, proposal, and associated documentation in coordination with CMEG.
  • Submitting the bid under CMEG’s name, with all required signatures executed by CMEG’s authorized officers.
  • Managing pre-award communications and administrative aspects of the procurement process where appropriate.
  • Ensuring that the submission complies with City of Alexandria procurement rules and Louisiana public procurement law (La. R.S. 38:201 et seq.).
SocialSculp does not perform hazardous waste work. SocialSculp does not act as a subcontractor for the performance of the contract. SocialSculp’s involvement is confined to procurement, bid management, and administrative facilitation so that CMEG can focus on delivering the services.

3. How This Partnership Benefits the Opportunity

SocialSculp’s mission is to connect qualified contractors with government and municipal opportunities they might not otherwise see or pursue. In this case:

  • Visibility: SocialSculp identified the City of Alexandria’s RFP #1962P and brought it to CMEG as a fit for CMEG’s expertise in household hazardous waste collection and disposal.
  • Procurement readiness: SocialSculp assists with bid preparation, documentation, and submission so that the proposal and quote are complete, compliant, and professionally presented.
  • Clear roles: CMEG remains solely responsible for all work under the contract; SocialSculp’s compensation is a procurement fee paid by CMEG, not by the City. The City’s costs are those set forth in CMEG’s quoted price.

We believe this structure supports the City’s goals by ensuring that a qualified, permitted contractor (CMEG) performs the work while procurement and submission are handled in an organized, compliant manner.

4. Legal and Contractual Foundation

The relationship between SocialSculp and CMEG is governed by a Procurement and Bid Submission Authority Agreement that, among other things:

  • Designates SocialSculp as CMEG’s authorized procurement agent for this solicitation.
  • Requires that the bid be submitted in CMEG’s name with signatures from CMEG’s authorized officers, in full compliance with Louisiana public procurement law and City of Alexandria requirements.
  • Confirms that CMEG retains all performance obligations and contractual responsibilities to the City.
  • Defines SocialSculp’s scope as procurement and bid management only, with no automatic role in performing the work.

This agreement is in place to ensure clarity, compliance, and a single point of performance responsibility (CMEG) toward the City.

5. Transparency and Compliance

  • The City may rely on the bid and quote as submitted by Clean Management Environmental Group, Inc. All technical representations, pricing, and commitments are made by CMEG.
  • SocialSculp has not made, and will not make, any representations to the City regarding CMEG’s capabilities, qualifications, or pricing without CMEG’s approval.
  • If the City’s solicitation or procurement rules require disclosure of a third-party procurement agent, this addendum serves as that disclosure. We are pleased to provide it in the spirit of full transparency.
