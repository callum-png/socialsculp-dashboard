Subcontractor Coordination

Event Day Responsibilities

1. Site Setup

Afternoon Before Event: Set up collection site

All setup charges, per diem, and travel costs included in "Set-Up" charge

2. Event Management

  • Oversee entire collection operation
  • Manage 18-20 staff members on site
  • Coordinate with City volunteers (20-30 provided by City)
  • Ensure proper traffic control and participant flow
  • Handle 500-750 cars expected at event

3. Material Handling

  • Unload materials from participants' vehicles
  • Segregate materials by type and hazard classification
  • Bulk latex paint using provided grills
  • Weigh containers before shipment
  • Ensure proper packaging and labeling

4. Safety & Compliance

  • Health and Safety Officer monitors all operations
  • Ensure all staff and volunteers wear appropriate PPE
  • Maintain safe working conditions
  • Follow all OSHA and regulatory requirements

Post-Event Responsibilities

1. Material Transportation

  • Assume generator status of all collected materials
  • Ship materials on hazardous waste manifest
  • Transport to EPA permitted TSDF with current RCRA Part B Permit
  • All transportation charges and fuel surcharges included in pricing

2. Documentation & Reporting

⚠️ Critical Deadline: 30 Days

  • Provide Certificates of Recycling and/or Disposal within 30 days of collection
  • Provide copies of manifest within 30 days of collection
  • Invoices will not be paid until these documents are provided

3. Disposal & Reclamation

Preferred Disposal Methods (in order):

  1. Recycling (most desirable)
  2. Fuel Incineration
  3. Incineration
  4. Landfill (least desirable)
