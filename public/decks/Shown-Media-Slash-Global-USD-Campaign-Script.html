Global USD Account

Sample product launch video script & strategy

Why this script & angle work

Angle: We position Slash as the bank that wants global businesses - "Your bank doesn't want your business. We raised millions so you can ditch them." - which taps into real frustration (frozen funds, shutdowns, banks treating you like a risk) and is inherently debatable. Viewers who've been burned will comment and share; skeptics will argue; both drive reach and conversation.

Retention & conversion: Short, punchy lines and a clear benefit stack (10 minutes, no LLC, 4% cash rewards, connect Shopify/Amazon/Stripe, wires to anyone) keep people watching. The CTA is a single line - "The link is in my bio" - so the next step is obvious. We lead with the hook and payoff early so even 30-second viewers get the big idea. The "Let's say you run a brand from France selling to the US..." scenario is specific enough to feel real without overloading detail, and makes the pain concrete.

Lead magnet at the end: Like the reference videos, we use a bonus at the very end: an AI agent that watches your bank account 24/7, cancels unwanted subscriptions, and flags double charges - and you don't need a Slash account to get it. Viewers retweet + comment "Slash" and we DM them the link. That's what drives the retweets and comments.

Conversation drivers: End with retweet + comment "Slash" so the algorithm gets engagement and DMs become a conversion path. Phrases like "no LLC formation bullshit," "10 minutes," and "Slash money works for you" are shareable. "Second-class citizens" vs. "superior banking partner" gives international founders a reason to tag peers and debate traditional banks vs. Slash.

Sample script (X / LinkedIn product launch video)

Hook
Your bank doesn’t want your business. We raised millions so you can ditch them.
Pain
If you’re building a business outside the US, you’ll know that traditional banks treat you like a risk instead of a customer. Frozen funds. Random shutdowns. Sometimes months waiting for paperwork.
Product
So we built the Global USD Account.
-
Let me show you how it works.
Scenario (audience-centered)
Let's say you run a brand from France selling to the US on Shopify and Amazon. You need to get paid in USD and pay your team and suppliers - but your bank just froze your last 2 payouts. You've also been waiting months for a US account that never approves. We built the Global USD Account for you.
How it works
You open an account in about 10 minutes. No US address. No SSN. No LLC formation bullshit. Just connect your payment platforms, and the money lands directly in your account. But the cool part is Slash money works for you. Your USD earns 4% cash rewards while it sits there. Wires go out to anyone, anytime. That’s a better banking experience than US companies get, built for 100+ countries.
Proof
We’re the modern digital bank for global businesses that are tired of asking permission. Thousands of businesses already use Slash. The ones that were done being treated like second-class citizens, but also businesses that just want a superior banking partner.
CTA
The link is in my bio.
