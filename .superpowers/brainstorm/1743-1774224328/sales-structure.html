How should the Sales section be organised?

Three different ways to structure what lives inside the Sales tab

A

Sub-tabs inside Sales

Sales tab has its own horizontal tab bar: Call Preps · Call Structures · Outreach Scripts. Each section is a separate view. Cleanest for future growth — easy to add new content types later.

B

Single page with sections

One scrollable Sales page. Call Preps at the top as cards, Call Structures below as a list, Outreach Scripts below that. Simpler — less navigation overhead, good when content volume is modest.

C

Sales absorbs CRM

Merge CRM + new Sales content under one Sales tab. Sub-tabs: Pipeline · Leads · Call Preps · Playbooks. Bigger scope — but all sales activity lives in one place.
