Integration should occur in the middle segment when you go to the gym or work out at home and use Fitbod.

Since your audience is already familiar with Fitbod from videos 1 and 2, this integration can be more organic and condensed—focus on the postpartum-specific workouts and time-saving aspect.

Integration Style (Natural Consumer Behavior):

Think about what a normal consumer would say: A normal consumer wouldn't overly promote or push the app. They would just naturally talk about it and how it was good for them—that's all. Think about what you would naturally do as a consumer if you were authentically trying to integrate it into your normal routine videos.

  • Natural Integration: Just naturally talk about Fitbod like you would talk about anything else that's part of your routine
  • Simple Talking Points: Maybe mention that you're a postpartum mom, Fitbod has postpartum-specific workouts that are personalized for your body, and the best part is they save you time so you can get back to your baby sooner. That's kind of all you need to say.
  • Keep It Natural: Try not to go overboard promoting the app—just naturally incorporate it into your normal vlog routine

  • Tag Fitbod: Tag @fitbod in the caption on both Instagram and TikTok
Key Message: The goal is to amalgamate and unite Fitbod with your normal routine vlog. This is what you normally do, and you're using Fitbod's postpartum workouts to save time and get back to your baby—Fitbod is just a part of that, and Fitbod powerhouses that.
