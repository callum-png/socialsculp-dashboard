Campaign Overview

⏰ Posting Deadline

Post Date: January 4, 2025

Highest Impact Day: Monday, January 5th (New Year's motivation peak)

Campaign Strategy

We're leveraging the New Year's resolution angle to tap into parallel consumerism. Your audience—postpartum moms, people in relationships, husbands, wives, parents—will see you making fitness a New Year's resolution and be inspired to do the same. By integrating Fitbod naturally into your routine, you'll showcase how accessible and easy at-home workouts can be, creating that "wow factor" of using Fitbod.

Campaign Goal

The goal is to inspire your audience to:

  • Get back into a fitness routine after the holidays
  • Feel confident starting or restarting strength training
  • Discover Fitbod as a personalized, easy-to-follow workout app
  • See that getting back into fitness doesn't have to be extreme, time-consuming, or perfect

Key Message: Fitbod helps people build real, sustainable strength for everyday life—perfect for busy moms like you.

Content Format & Style

Video Concept

"Day in the Life" or lifestyle vlog video showcasing your New Year's resolutions as a busy postpartum mom.

Video Structure:

  • Length: 1-2 minutes max (not 4-5 minutes—people won't watch the full video)
  • Pacing: Slightly faster than your normal videos
  • Workout Segment: Middle segment of the video (around 30-second mark, or 30-45 seconds if longer video)

Content Angle: Film throughout the day showing you accomplishing your New Year's resolutions—like a January challenge turning into a yearly habit. For example:

  • Starting the day with healthy food choices
  • Midday workout routine using Fitbod
  • Showcasing how Fitbod fits into your busy schedule

Tone: Think less "New Year, new me" and more "This is how I realistically stay strong and confident." New Year's resolutions are just a part of becoming a better person—not a complete transformation.

Target Audience Alignment: Your audience should see a realistic postpartum mom routine. What would a busy mom actually go through? She'd be almost too busy to fit in a workout, but she still does it because Fitbod makes it accessible.

Integration Guidelines

⏱️ Integration Timing (Critical)

Integration should occur around the 30-second mark (within the 30-45 second range) to align with your average watch time on day-in-the-life/lifestyle vlog videos.

This ensures viewers don't scroll before the integration happens.

Integration Length: 5-10 seconds max (ideally 10 seconds, leaning towards concise)

💬 Dialogue Approach: Role Model (Not Promotional)

Critical: Do NOT overtly promote the app. Take a role model approach—share your organic, authentic experience. Your viewers should want to follow your lead, not feel like they're being sold to.

Key: You're vocalizing your real experience, not telling people to use Fitbod. This creates the parallel consumerism effect where your audience naturally wants to follow your example.

🎬 Integration Visual Style

Show Yourself Working Out: Display the exercises you're doing postpartum while working out

App Visuals (Key): Instead of just text overlays, show cropped screenshots/GIFs from inside the Fitbod app showing the workout routine Fitbod gave you

  • Show the Fitbod app interface
  • Display visuals of the inner Fitbod app's workout routine
  • Use captions (great for engagement and accessibility)

Why This Matters: Showing the actual Fitbod app visuals helps pregnant viewers see how the app works and incentivizes them to sign up too.

🗣️ Dialogue Guide (In Your Own Words)

Something like this (not verbatim, stay authentic):

"Being two months postpartum, I made it my New Year's resolution to prioritize my health again because during pregnancy, most workouts didn't adjust to my body. But this app called Fitbod did [show app] and I've still been using it postpartum because it gives me a personalized workout and saves me so much time."

[show exercises] "This is what it planned for me today."

Important: Don't implore or tell viewers to use it—that makes it feel like an ad. The role model approach is much stronger for parallel consumerism. Make it concise, natural, and authentic to your voice.

Integration Elements:

  • First Frame: Show you and your baby in the first 1-3 seconds—especially with emotional moments like baby crying, as viewers stay and watch
  • Organic & Authentic: Not overly promotional—share your real experience with Fitbod
  • Show the App Visually: Display cropped screenshots/GIFs from inside the Fitbod app showing your workout routine
  • Mention Fitbod Naturally: Mention the app name when you're working out
  • Captions: Use captions to show the exercises and highlight key points
  • Tag Fitbod: Tag @fitbod in the caption on both Instagram and TikTok
Key Message Focus:
  • Fitbod gave you a customizable routine specifically for being pregnant
  • You're still using it postpartum because it works for busy moms
  • It's accessible, personalized, and feasible—no matter your situation
  • This approach also incentivizes pregnant viewers to sign up

Content Themes (Choose What Feels Natural)

These themes are for your reference—pick what aligns best with your content. You don't need to cover everything.

1. Getting Lean & Defined

Focus on confidence, muscle definition, feeling strong—not perfection. Post-holiday reset, feeling more toned and confident, following a structured plan instead of guessing.

2. Building Muscle & Getting Stronger

Strength as empowerment—especially as a mom. Lifting heavier, feeling capable and strong to take care of your family, progressive strength over time.

3. Staying Healthy & Fit

Fitness as a sustainable, long-term habit. Mental health and energy, long-term consistency, fitness that supports family life.

4. Losing Weight Sustainably

Focus on strength and body recomposition, feeling better—nothing extreme. Building muscle to support fat loss, letting go of scale obsession, avoiding crash diets or cardio-only routines.
