How It Works

Not Influencer Ads.

Creator Identification

We find micro and mid-tier creators whose audience exactly matches your app's target user — by niche, demographic, and content behaviour. No spray-and-pray.

Brief Engineering

We write content briefs that embed your app's value proposition into a native format — the hook is the content, not the ad. Creators post authentically. Audiences convert.

Test → Validate → Scale

We seed small first. Validate which angles convert. Then double down — deploying the winning brief across dozens of creators in the same demo simultaneously.

Rizz App

Dating & Social.
Creator Seeding Wins Here.

Social and dating apps are the category where creator trust matters most. An authentic creator recommendation outperforms paid ads by a wide margin — audiences install because someone they follow uses it, not because they saw a banner.

Brief creators in the dating/confidence/social skills niche — "how I improved my conversations" content naturally embeds the app
Lifestyle creators showing "apps I use to meet people / build social skills" — high-trust, native discovery
Test multiple angles across 10–20 creators simultaneously, scale the winner across 50–100+
Expected CPM: $1–2 vs $20–40 for equivalent paid targeting
Cantina

AI Character Creation.
Entertainment Creators Own This.

Cantina lets users build, customise, and share AI-powered character bots — then create video messages and set them loose. The virality is built in. Creator seeding accelerates what's already native to how people share this kind of content.

Brief entertainment and AI creators around "meet my AI character" and "I built a bot that..." — the content format is inherently shareable
Gaming, animation, and Gen Z lifestyle creators — audiences that respond to character creation and creative tools
"Rate my bot" and character reveal challenges — engineered for duet and stitch behaviour, extending campaign reach organically
The product IS the content — creators don't need to force an integration, their audience becomes users because the bot is the hook
