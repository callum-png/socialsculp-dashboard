Why Video 1 Overperformed

Video 1 drove 857K views at $3.50 CPM and set the benchmark:

  • First-frame emotional hook: Mom and baby having a genuine reaction in the open—grabbed attention and improved retention.
  • Mom carrying baby in the first frame: Quickly signaled “motherhood + real life,” matched her audience, and kept viewers watching.
  • Retention-focused open: Strong first few seconds drove watch time and algorithm pickup, so more reach and views.

This matches Gabriella’s organic hits (e.g. ~600K-view TikTok on postpartum stomach / “still there”). Underperformers usually lack that clear emotional moment from mom or baby and feel like generic lifestyle clips.

Key Takeaways & Recommendations

If we renew with Gabriella Annalisa:

  • Lead with a first-frame emotional hook (mom/baby reaction, relatable moment) to maximize retention and reach.
  • Optimize for retention in the first few seconds—avoid generic opens.
  • Use “day in the life” storytelling—visually and in copy—so Fitbod feels natural, not ad-like.
  • Add variety (scenarios, hooks, formats) while keeping the emotional thread that works for her audience.

Partnership: A long-standing deal with Gabriella Annalisa is on the table if the campaign attributes well for Fitbod. Renewal should factor in backend attribution, not just CPM/views.

Broader strategy: Cycle One was single-creator due to budget. Phase Two should widen the rollout (Jeremiah Jones, TrenTwins, Gharilla, other fitness creators) to diversify, gather more data, and avoid over-reliance on one partner.
